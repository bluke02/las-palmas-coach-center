Pitcher and catcher position changes

These Little League International baseball rules determine whether a player may move between pitcher and catcher during the same day. Track innings caught and pitches delivered independently.

SituationWhat the rule requires
Catcher to pitcherA player who catches four or more innings in a game is not eligible to pitch later that calendar day.
Catcher (age 14 and under) to pitcher, 3 innings or fewer caughtThe player may pitch, but may not return to catcher after delivering 21 or more pitches. If the player reaches 20 pitches while facing a batter, they may finish that batter and retain catcher eligibility until the at-bat or half-inning ends, or they are removed.
Catcher (age 15–16) to pitcher, 3 innings or fewer caughtThe player may pitch, but may not return to catcher after delivering 31 or more pitches. If the player reaches 30 pitches while facing a batter, the same live-batter exception applies.
Pitcher to catcherIf a player delivers 41 or more pitches, they may not play catcher for the remainder of that day unless covered by the live-batter threshold exception in the official rule. Confirm the applicable age and division rule before moving the player.
Pitcher removed from moundOnce removed from the mound, a pitcher may not return as pitcher. In Intermediate (50/70), Junior, and Senior divisions only, a pitcher who remains in the game may move to another defensive position and return to pitch once later in the game.

Source: Little League International Regular Season Pitching Rules. Ask the umpire or league officials about any situation not covered here.

Pregame coach checklist

Players & lineup

  • Confirm attendance.
  • Complete the batting order.
  • Assign defensive positions.
  • Identify emergency substitutes.

Pitchers & catchers

  • Confirm pitcher eligibility and rest.
  • Review pitch-count limits.
  • Review catcher-to-pitcher restrictions.
  • Inspect catcher equipment.

Rules & safety

  • Know time, run-limit, mercy-rule, and curfew procedures.
  • Confirm stealing and local modifications.
  • Have first-aid supplies available.
  • Inspect the playing surface and supervise the dugout.

The Las Palmas standard

Every coach should leave the field knowing every player was respected, developed, safe, and given an opportunity to compete. Winning is a goal; developing players is the mission.
