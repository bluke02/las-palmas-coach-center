Before, during, and after the game

Before

  • Inspect field and equipment.
  • Confirm available players.
  • Verify pitching eligibility.
  • Create a participation plan.
  • Assign scorekeeper and tracker.

During

  • Supervise player areas.
  • Track pitching and participation.
  • Provide water breaks.
  • Stop for unsafe conditions.
  • Model respect for umpires.

After

  • Reconcile pitching records.
  • Report injuries or incidents.
  • Collect equipment.
  • Help restore the field.
  • Send the next-event reminder.
Emergency note: Follow the Las Palmas emergency action plan and instructions from league officials. This portal does not provide medical diagnosis or replace emergency services.
