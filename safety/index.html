Safe, Organized Game Days

Practical, player-first support for volunteer baseball and softball coaches.

Game-day checklist

Before

Inspect field and equipment; verify eligibility and helpers.

During

Supervise, track participation, hydrate, and stop for unsafe conditions.

After

Reconcile records, report incidents, and collect equipment.
