End-of-practice team games

Use one of these short, low-pressure games to finish practice with teamwork, communication, and a positive shared experience. Keep score only when it helps the group; celebrate effort, encouragement, and smart decisions.

Relay Challenge

Setup: Create equal teams and a short relay lane with a ball, cone, or base at the turn.

Play: Each player completes the movement, returns, and hands off safely to the next teammate. Try a second round with a new skill, such as a ground-ball pickup or accurate throw to a target.

Team-building focus: Players encourage every teammate and value clean handoffs over speed.

Keep-It-Up

Setup: Groups form a circle with one ball. Use a coach toss for younger players or controlled catches for older players.

Play: The group tries to complete a target number of catches without dropping the ball. Reset kindly and let players choose a new strategy.

Team-building focus: Communication, shared goals, and helping teammates recover after a mistake.

Four-Corner Cooperation

Setup: Place four cones in a square with a team at each corner and one ball in play.

Play: Teams move the ball around the square using accurate throws. Add a point when all four corners complete a clean catch, then change direction or add a ground ball.

Team-building focus: Clear targets, active listening, and recognizing the pass that sets up a teammate.

Play: Use commands such as ready position, shuffle, freeze, point to a target, or celebrate a teammate. Keep everyone in the game by resetting rather than eliminating players.

Team-building focus: Listening, focus, laughter, and including every player.

Celebration Scrimmage

Setup: Play a short small-sided game with a team goal such as three completed plays, three loud calls, or helping a teammate get ready.

Play: Pause after successful moments so the team can name the communication or effort that made the play work. Rotate positions and responsibilities often.

Team-building focus: Shared recognition, sportsmanship, and learning that success is more than the final score.

5 minutes

Team Cheer Circle

Setup: Bring players together in a circle with equipment safely stored.

Play: Each player names one teammate's helpful action from practice. Finish with a team cheer chosen by the players.

Team-building focus: Belonging, gratitude, and ending practice on a positive note.

Coaching cue: Avoid elimination games and public rankings. Adjust distance, equipment, and rules so every player can contribute and finish feeling successful.
