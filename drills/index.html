Hit the Bucket

Purpose: Accuracy, focus, and friendly team scoring.

Setup: Place a bucket or large target at an age-appropriate distance. Players earn a point for a controlled throw that reaches the target.

Safety: Keep throws short enough for sound mechanics. Move the target only after success.

Four Corners

Purpose: Catch, transfer, footwork, and communication.

Setup: Set four cones in a square. Players throw clockwise, then reverse direction. Add a ground-ball entry or timed clean circuit.

Safety: Require a ready target and verbal call before every throw.

fieldingall

Ground-Ball Gates

Purpose: Athletic setup, two-hand fielding, and accurate finish.

Setup: Create cone gates. Roll a ball through each gate; the player fields, sets feet, and throws to a target.

Safety: Use rolled balls first and keep players outside throwing lanes.

hittingall

Tee Zones

Purpose: Contact quality and directional hitting.

Setup: Mark three safe field zones. The hitter earns points for hard contact through the called zone.

Safety: One bat swings at a time; helmets on; everyone else behind a safety line.

Traffic-Light Base Running

Purpose: Listening, stopping, and base recognition.

Setup: Green means run, yellow means athletic stop, and red means freeze. Progress to running through first and rounding.

Safety: Space runners to avoid collisions.

21 Outs

Purpose: Team defense and focus under manageable pressure.

Setup: The team earns an out for a clean field-and-throw sequence. Reset the count or remove one point after an error.

Safety: Celebrate communication and decisions, not only perfect execution.

Fly-Ball Communication

Purpose: Tracking, priority, communication, and backup responsibilities.

Setup: Start with tennis balls or soft toss. Require a loud call and a backup player on every repetition.

Safety: No blind over-the-shoulder collisions. Spread groups widely.

Chaos Catch

Purpose: Reaction, tracking, and athletic movement.

Setup: In pairs, point toward a cone before a safe toss. Progress from predictable to reactive movement.

Safety: Scale distance and speed to confidence.

Mini Game

Purpose: Decision-making through small-sided play.

Setup: Play 3v3 or 4v4 in a reduced area with one teaching rule such as force at first or hitting behind a runner.

Safety: Use soft balls when space is limited.

Rapid Fire Soft Toss

Purpose: Repeatable setup and compact contact.

Setup: Two hitters alternate at separate nets or safe stations while helpers feed underhand tosses.

Safety: Use screens and strict separation between hitting areas.

Cutoff Relay Race

Purpose: Relay alignment, quick transfers, and communication.

Setup: Teams move the ball through two cutoff players to a target. Score clean technique before speed.

Safety: Set parallel lanes far enough apart.

Catcher Block and Recover

Purpose: Safe blocking, recovery, and locating the ball.

Setup: Use tennis balls or reduced-speed feeds. Block, find the ball, and make a controlled throw.

Safety: Full catcher equipment for regulation balls; avoid high-velocity feeds.
