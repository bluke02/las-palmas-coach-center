Baseball division pathway

Local-label note: Minors A and Minors B are local operating labels. Coaches should confirm the exact format with Las Palmas before planning games or teaching division-specific tactics.

Baseball

Tee Ball

Coach focus: fun, fitness, movement, safe bat habits, throwing, catching, and base recognition.

Do not assume: rules from another division, travel program, school team, or prior season apply.

Questions: Ask the division director about pitching format, batting order, run limits, stealing or leaving-base rules, bunting, game length, substitution, and any local ground rules.
