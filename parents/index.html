Team Parent Center

Family coordinator

Manage reminders, volunteer schedules, permitted snack coordination, celebrations, photo permissions, and lost-and-found.

Dugout helper

Support helmets, batting order, hydration prompts, bench safety, and equipment count after completing required approval.

Scorekeeper or tracker

Track the official book, required pitching records, substitutions, and participation plan. Reconcile records instead of guessing.

10-minute family meeting

  1. Development, safety, belonging, and fun.
  2. Calendar, attendance, arrival, pickup, and weather communication.
  3. Equipment and uniform expectations.
  4. Volunteer roles and clearance.
  5. Sportsmanship and private concern-resolution process.
  6. How confidential, injury, and accessibility information should be shared through approved channels.

Boundary: Team parents help with administration. Lineups, player discipline, medical decisions, and confidential concerns remain with authorized league personnel.
